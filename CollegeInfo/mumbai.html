Top Engineering Colleges in Mumbai 2026

A comprehensive directory of the best engineering institutions in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, and Thane. Updated for 2026.

Published by MSBTE Notes & Info Team ∙ February 11, 2026

Mumbai, the financial capital of India, is a powerhouse of technical education and industrial innovation. With iconic institutions like VJTI (established 1887) and ICT Mumbai leading the charge, the city offers a unique blend of heritage and cutting-edge research.

Students in Mumbai benefit from unmatched proximity to India's largest corporate headquarters, financial districts, and thriving tech startups in Powai and Andheri. The region's engineering colleges are renowned for their rigorous academic standards, strong alumni networks, and exceptional placement records in sectors ranging from FinTech and IT to manufacturing and chemical engineering. The directory below covers top-tier autonomous, government-aided, and private institutes across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane, and Palghar.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the No. 1 Engineering College in Mumbai? expand_more
VJTI (Veermata Jijabai Technological Institute) is widely considered the top engineering institute in Mumbai, known for its history, autonomy, and strong alumni network.
What is the cutoff for VJTI Computer Science? expand_more
Cutoffs are very high, often above 99.5 percentile in MHT-CET for General category students in Computer Engineering.
Are there good colleges in Navi Mumbai? expand_more
Yes, colleges like Fr. Agnel (Vashi), Ramrao Adik (DY Patil), and SIES Nerul are excellent choices with good placement records.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which university are Mumbai colleges affiliated to? expand_more
Most colleges are affiliated to Mumbai University (MU), while ICT, VJTI, and SPIT are autonomous.
What is the average engineering fee in Mumbai? expand_more
Engineering fees in Mumbai range from ₹15,000 for government-aided seats (VJTI, SPCE) to ₹2.5 Lakhs per year for top private institutes.
Do Mumbai engineering colleges provide hostel facilities? expand_more
Yes, institutes like VJTI, ICT, and SPIT offer hostel facilities, though seats are limited and merit-based.
