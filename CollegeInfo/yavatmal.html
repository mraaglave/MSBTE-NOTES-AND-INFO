Top Engineering Colleges in Yavatmal 2026

A complete list of engineering institutes in Yavatmal, offering excellent education in Vidarbha.

Published by MSBTE Notes & Info Team ∙ February 11, 2026

Yavatmal is known for its cotton production and educational institutions. The Government College of Engineering, Yavatmal stands out as a premier institute in the region.

Along with private players like Jawaharlal Darda Institute of Engineering and Technology, the district offers quality technical education affiliated with Amravati University.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a government engineering college in Yavatmal? expand_more
Yes, Government College of Engineering, Yavatmal is a well-known government institute in the district.
What is the average engineering fee in Yavatmal? expand_more
Engineering fees in Yavatmal range from ₹24,000 for government seats to ₹90,000 per year for private institutes like JDIET.
Do Yavatmal engineering colleges provide hostel facilities? expand_more
Yes, Government College of Engineering, Yavatmal provides separate hostel facilities for boys and girls.
