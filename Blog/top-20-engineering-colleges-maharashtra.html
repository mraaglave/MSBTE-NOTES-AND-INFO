Top 20 Engineering Colleges in Maharashtra for MHT CET Aspirants (Detailed Guide)

Choosing the right engineering college is one of the most important decisions in a student's academic journey. Maharashtra is home to several prestigious government, government-aided, autonomous, and private engineering institutions that offer excellent academics, modern infrastructure, strong industry collaborations, and impressive placement opportunities.

Top 20 Engineering Colleges Breakdown

1. Veermata Jijabai Technological Institute (VJTI), Mumbai

Cutoff Percentile: ~ 97.00%
Established: 1887
Type: Govt. Autonomous
Location: Matunga, Mumbai

Established in 1887, Veermata Jijabai Technological Institute (VJTI) is one of the oldest engineering institutes in Asia and is widely regarded as Maharashtra's most prestigious government engineering college. Supported by the Government of Maharashtra and affiliated with the University of Mumbai, it occupies a prime spot in technical education. You can check our guide on the Top 5 Engineering Colleges in Maharashtra for details.

Placement & Fees:
  • Highest Package: ₹54 LPA (2024-25 season)
  • Recruiters: 300+ companies participated
  • NIRF 2025 Rank: 101-150 Band
  • General Category Fees: ~₹72,000 per year
Key Disciplines:

Computer Engineering, Information Technology, Electronics Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Production Engineering, and Textile Technology.

Final Thoughts

Maharashtra offers an exceptional range of engineering colleges catering to different academic interests, career goals, and financial backgrounds. Government institutions such as VJTI, GCE Karad, GCE Amravati, GCE Nagpur, and GCOEARA provide world-class education at highly affordable fees. Government-aided colleges like WCE Sangli, SPCE Mumbai, and SGGS Nanded combine academic excellence with strong industry exposure.

For students interested in software engineering, artificial intelligence, and information technology, colleges such as PICT Pune, PCCOE Pune, DJSCE Mumbai, VESIT Mumbai, VIT Pune, TSEC Mumbai, FCRIT Navi Mumbai, K J Somaiya Institute of Technology, PCCOER Pune, AISSMS IOIT Pune, and I²IT Pune consistently deliver excellent placement outcomes and industry-ready education.

While cutoff percentiles are an important factor during admission, students should also evaluate placement records, faculty quality, internship opportunities, campus infrastructure, research facilities, location, specialization, alumni network, and long-term career prospects before finalizing their college preferences during the CAP counselling process. A well-informed decision can significantly influence both academic growth and future professional success.

FAQ — Engineering Admissions in Maharashtra

Which is the highest-ranked engineering college in Maharashtra through MHT CET?

Veermata Jijabai Technological Institute (VJTI), Mumbai is widely regarded as the top college in Maharashtra for MHT CET aspirants, with cutoffs usually around the 97+ percentile.

What are the top engineering colleges in Pune for Computer Science?

Pune Institute of Computer Technology (PICT), Vishwakarma Institute of Technology (VIT), and Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE) are among the best private autonomous options in Pune.

Are autonomous engineering colleges better than non-autonomous ones?

Yes, autonomous colleges design their own curriculum, conduct exams independently, and frequently update their courses to align with industry requirements, often leading to better placements.
