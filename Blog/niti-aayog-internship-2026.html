NITI Aayog Internship 2026: Your Gateway to Policy Making

Published by MSBTE Notes Team ∙ January 29, 2026 ∙ 5 min read

The NITI Aayog Internship Scheme 2026 offers a prestigious opportunity for undergraduate, postgraduate, and research scholars to engage with India’s premier policy-making institution. Designed to provide hands-on experience in public policy, research, and governance, this program is a significant career milestone for those looking to contribute to national development.

🚀 Why Apply?

Gain direct exposure to governance and policy frameworks, work under top bureaucracts, and enhance your resume with a prestigious government internship.

Core Eligibility Criteria

The internship is strictly merit-based, with specific academic thresholds for different levels of education:

Category Academic Requirements
Undergraduate Completed 4th sem (2nd year) with 85% in Class 12.
Postgraduate Completed 2nd sem (1st year) with 70% in Graduation.
Research/PhD Secured at least 70% or equivalent in graduation.
Recent Graduates Eligible within 6 months of result declaration (70%+).

Key Program Details

  • ₹ Stipend: The internship is unpaid.
  • 🕒 Duration: Minimum 6 weeks, Maximum 6 months.
  • 📍 Location: Primarily on-site at NITI Aayog, New Delhi.
  • 💻 Tools: Interns must bring their own laptops.

How to Apply for the 2026 Cycle

The application window is highly specific:

  • Window: Portal active only from the 1st to 10th of every month.
  • Timing: Apply at least 2 months in advance (e.g., Apply in Feb for April start).

Documents Required:

  • Updated Resume (PDF)
  • Academic Transcripts/Marksheets
  • Government ID (Aadhaar/PAN)
  • Statement of Purpose (SOP) - 500-1000 words
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) upon joining

Selection Process

Applications are scrutinized by the respective Vertical/Division heads. Since the selection is highly competitive, ensure your SOP highlights your research skills and commitment to public policy. Only shortlisted candidates are contacted via email.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is the NITI Aayog Internship paid?

A: No, the internship is unpaid. Interns must bear their own expenses for travel and accommodation in Delhi.

Q: Can I apply if I am in my 1st year of college?

A: No, for undergraduate students, you must have completed your 4th semester (2nd year) or appeared for the 4th-semester exams.

Q: When is the application link active?

A: The online portal is open only from the 1st to the 10th of every month.
