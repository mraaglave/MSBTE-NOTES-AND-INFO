🗓️ FINAL MSBTE Winter 2025 Theory Examination Timetable

Published by Mr. Patil ∙ September 25, 2025

The FINAL official MSBTE Winter 2025 theory exam timetable is here. Download the PDF, check exam dates for all regular and backlog papers, and get essential preparation tips.

The Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE) has released the FINAL timetable for the Winter 2025 theory examinations. This guide provides a complete overview of the exam schedule, a direct link to download the official PDF, and key tips to help you prepare effectively for both regular and backlog papers.

How to Check Your Timetable Online

  1. Visit the official MSBTE portal: online.msbte.co.in.
  2. Navigate to the "Examination" or "Timetable" section on the homepage.
  3. Find the link for the "Winter 2025 Theory Exam Timetable".
  4. You can search for your institute code, course code, or download the complete PDF.
  5. Verify your subject codes and exam dates carefully.

Key Exam Preparation Tips

  • Prioritize Subjects: Identify difficult subjects and allocate more study time to them.
  • Solve Past Papers: Practice with previous year's question papers to understand the pattern.
  • Focus on High-Weightage Topics: Check the syllabus for topic-wise marks distribution.
  • Stay Healthy: Ensure you get enough sleep and maintain a balanced diet to stay focused.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: When will the MSBTE Winter 2025 theory exams start?
A: The theory exams are scheduled to begin in November 2025.

Q: Is this timetable final?
A: Yes, this is the FINAL timetable released by MSBTE. However, it's always a good practice to check the official website for any last-minute circulars or changes.

Q: What should I do if two of my exams are on the same day?
A: Immediately contact your institute's exam cell. They will guide you on the procedure for reporting clashes.

Final Words

With the MSBTE Winter 2025 final timetable now available, it's time to structure your study plan and begin your revisions. Use this schedule to your advantage and prepare well for your exams.

Disclaimer: This information is based on the final timetable released by MSBTE. Students are advised to always cross-verify with the official MSBTE website for any further updates or circulars.
