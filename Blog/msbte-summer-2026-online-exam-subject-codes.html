MSBTE Summer 2026 Online Exam – Complete Subject Code List for All 6 Phases

Published by MSBTE Notes & Info Team · April 10, 2026

If you are a MSBTE diploma student appearing for the Summer 2026 online exam, use this complete subject-code reference to find your phase and exam dates across all six phases for K-Scheme and I-Scheme programs.

MSBTE has released its official online exam schedule for the Summer 2026 session via Circular No. 42/Online Exam/12 dated 8 April 2026. Below is the complete subject-code reference for all six phases. Cross-check your subject code and note your phase carefully. For overview dates and important board instructions, see our MSBTE Summer 2026 Online Exam Schedule article. If your subject is mechanical materials, check the Phase 6 Rescheduling Alert.
