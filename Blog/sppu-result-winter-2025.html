🔍 How to Check SPPU Result (Step-by-Step)

  1. Visit Official Result Portal
    Go to onlineresults.unipune.ac.in
  2. Select Your Exam Type
    Choose between "UG (Undergraduate)" or "PG (Postgraduate)" from the categories
  3. Select Winter Session 2025
    Choose the exam session from the dropdown menu
  4. Enter Your PRN Number
    Input your PRN (Permanent Registration Number) from your I-Card or Hall Ticket
  5. Click "Show Result"
    Your result will display on screen with subject-wise marks
  6. Download PDF Marksheet
    Click "Download PDF" button to save your marksheet for future use

😟 Result Not Showing? Try These:

  • Clear browser cache and cookies, then try again
  • Use a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Edge)
  • Try using mobile data instead of WiFi
  • Check during off-peak hours (early morning or late night)
  • Contact your college examination cell if the issue persists for more than 24 hours

📝 SPPU Revaluation Process 2025

If you're not satisfied with your marks, you can apply for revaluation where your answer sheets are re-checked by a different examiner.

👥 Who Should Apply for Revaluation?

  • Students who failed by a small margin (5-10 marks)
  • Those confident about their answers and performance
  • Students expecting higher marks based on their preparation
  • NOT recommended if you're unsure — marks can decrease!

💰 Revaluation Fees

  • Per Subject: Check official SPPU circular
  • Application Window: Usually 7-10 days after result
  • Payment Mode: Online via SPPU portal

📈 Important Points

  • Processing Time: 2-3 weeks
  • Result Change: Marks can increase, decrease, or stay same
  • Final Decision: Revaluation result is final

⚡ Pro Tips for Revaluation

  • Apply only if you're confident about your performance
  • Check the official deadline carefully — late applications are not accepted
  • Keep payment receipt and application confirmation safe
  • Don't apply blindly — evaluate your actual paper performance first

🎁 SPPU Grace Marks Policy

Grace marks are additional marks awarded by the university to help borderline students pass. Here's what you need to know:

✅ Who Gets Grace Marks?

  • Students who are short by a small margin to pass
  • Final year students (priority given)
  • Grace marks are automatically applied — no separate application needed
  • Applied as per university norms and regulations

📊 Grace Marks Details

  • Application: Automatic
  • Eligibility: Borderline cases
  • Priority: Final year students

📝 Important Notes

  • Grace marks shown separately in marksheet
  • Not applicable for practical exams
  • University discretion applies

🎓 SPPU: University Highlights

Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU), formerly the University of Pune, is located in Pune, Maharashtra and is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC).

📚 Key Information

The university was established in the year 1949 under the Pune University Act passed by the Bombay legislature.

SPPU offers diploma, UG, PG, and doctoral programs in various specializations. This University offers approximately 270 courses in the Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences, Commerce, Science, Education, and others.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: When is SPPU Winter 2025 Result declared?

A: The SPPU Winter Session 2025 Result has been declared. Students can check their results at onlineresults.unipune.ac.in.

Q: How to download SPPU marksheet PDF?

A: Visit the official result portal, enter your PRN, view your result, and click the "Download PDF" button to save your marksheet.

Q: What is the SPPU revaluation deadline?

A: Revaluation applications are typically accepted for 7-10 days after result declaration. Check the official SPPU circular for exact dates.

Q: Does SPPU provide grace marks?

A: Yes, SPPU provides grace marks for borderline cases, especially to final year students. Grace marks are applied automatically.

Q: What to do if my SPPU result shows ATKT?

A: ATKT means you have backlogs. You can appear for those subjects in the next examination cycle while continuing your current semester.
