Engineering Admission 2026: Institute-Level Round Strictly Merit-Based, CET Cell Guidelines

Published by MSBTE Notes & Info Team · September 6, 2026 · ⏱️ 5 min read

The Maharashtra State Common Entrance Test Cell (CET Cell) has issued stringent guidelines for Institute-Level Admissions and Against-CAP vacancies for A.Y. 2026–27. Colleges must follow a 100% transparent merit-based process with mandatory daily reporting and government-appointed observers.

⚡ TL;DR: Key Takeaways

The Maharashtra CET Cell has made it mandatory that all post-CAP Institute-Level Admissions for Engineering (B.E./B.Tech & DSE) 2026–27 must be conducted strictly according to merit. Colleges are required to submit daily admission status reports on the official CET portal, and independent state observers will monitor proceedings to prevent donations, backdoor allotments, and verbal assurance scams.

1. Official Circular Status & Background

With the completion of the Centralized Admission Process (CAP Rounds) for First Year Engineering (B.E./B.Tech) and Direct Second Year Engineering (DSE), the Maharashtra State Common Entrance Test Cell has issued critical guidelines for Institute-Level Admissions for the Academic Year 2026–27.

📌 Official Circular Overview:

  • Issuing Authority: State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra State
  • Status: Official & Mandatory
  • Target Academic Year: 2026–2027
  • Applicable Courses: First Year Engineering (B.E./B.Tech) & Direct Second Year Engineering (DSE)
  • Official CET Cell Portal: https://cetcell.mahacet.org/

Historically, post-CAP vacancy rounds have been prone to student confusion, lack of clear merit lists, and instances where deserving students missed out on seats in favor of informal seat claims. The 2026 directives establish an ironclad framework ensuring that every vacant seat in government, autonomous, and private unaided colleges is allotted with maximum transparency.

2. The 3 Core Pillars of 2026 Guidelines

The State CET Cell has enforced three strict mechanisms that all participating engineering institutions must adhere to:

✅

100% Merit-Based

Seats must be filled purely according to the merit rank (MHT-CET / JEE Main percentile for First Year, or Diploma aggregate marks for DSE). Colleges cannot jump merit ranks under any pretext.

📊

Daily Reporting

Institutes are legally required to upload daily admission reporting onto the central CET Cell portal. This ensures every seat allotment and cancellation is permanently tracked in real time.

👀

State Observers

Independent government-appointed observers and flying squads will inspect institute-level admission rounds across campuses to prevent irregularities, verify merit displays, and resolve on-spot issues.

3. Against-CAP (ACAP) vs. Institute-Level (IL) Quota

A common query among students researching Top Engineering Colleges in Maharashtra is the exact difference between Against-CAP seats and Institutional Quota seats. Here is the official breakdown:

Seat Type Seat Origin Allotment Basis Fee Norms
Against-CAP (ACAP) Vacancies CAP seats that remain vacant due to non-reporting or cancellation after CAP Round 3 Strict General Merit list of registered applicants Standard Fee Regulating Authority (FRA) approved college fee
Institutional Level (IL) Quota Permitted up to 20% sanctioned intake in private unaided engineering institutes Merit list of candidates who submitted college application forms Standard tuition fee approved by FRA (No capitation fees allowed)

4. Step-by-Step Guide for Students Without a CAP Seat

If you did not secure a seat during CAP rounds or vacated your previous seat hoping for higher-tier institutes or in-demand branches (such as Computer Science, AI & Data Science, or Electronics & Telecommunication), follow this protocol:

  1. Download the Final Vacancy Matrix: Check the official CET Cell website immediately after CAP Round 3 reporting ends to view college-wise, branch-wise, and category-wise vacant seat matrices.
  2. Check Institute Websites & Public Notices: Every college is mandated to publish its spot round admission schedule, registration deadline, merit list display date, and counseling venue in newspapers and on their official websites.
  3. Fill the Institutional Application Form: Submit the application form (either online on the college portal or physically at the admissions office) along with the required processing fee before the institutional cutoff time.
  4. Verify Your Name on the Published Merit List: Before counseling commences, colleges must display the provisional and final institutional merit lists on notice boards and online portals. Check your merit position carefully.
  5. Attend In-Person Counseling on the Day of Spot Rounds: Candidates must be physically present when their merit number is called. If an open seat in your desired branch is available, you must claim it immediately.
  6. Evaluate Degree Options: When picking between university affiliated and autonomous institutes, review our detailed guide on BE vs BTech differences in Maharashtra to make an informed choice.

5. Fee Structure & Scholarship Realities (EBC/TFWS)

One of the most frequent dilemmas students face is whether government scholarship concessions apply to institutional admissions. Here are the ground realities:

💡 Important Fee & Scholarship Rules:

  • Institutional Quota (20% Seats): As per state government guidelines, government scholarships (such as Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulk Shishyavrutti Yojna - EBC, Social Welfare freeships, or TFWS concessions) are not applicable for admissions taken under the institutional quota. Students must pay the full FRA-approved college tuition and development fees.
  • Against-CAP (ACAP) Vacancies: Even though ACAP seats originate from CAP rounds, most colleges require candidates to pay full open fees at the time of admission, subject to subsequent government notifications or DBT reimbursement approvals.
  • No Cash or Under-the-Table Fees: All fee payments must be conducted through Demand Drafts (DD), Net Banking, or official UPI payment gateways. Cash payments are prohibited.

6. ⚠️ Critical Warnings: Middlemen & Verbal Assurances

🛑 Do Not Fall for Admission Traps:

  • Beware of Touts & Unofficial Educational Agents: No third-party agent or consultancy has the legal authority to allocate or guarantee engineering seats. Every vacant seat must be allotted through the open merit call.
  • No Verbal Assurances: Never pay tokens or advance money based solely on oral promises like "your seat is reserved, just attend the round". Confirmation is only valid when an official allotment letter is issued via the CET portal.
  • Demand Merit Transparency: You have the legal right to inspect the complete merit roster. If a college calls candidates out of turn, report it immediately to the State Observers stationed on campus.
  • Official Grievance Registration: If you observe irregularities, lodge an official complaint on the CET Cell Grievance Portal with relevant documentary evidence.

7. Document Checklist & Retention Certificate

Because institutional and spot rounds move rapidly on the day of counseling, failure to produce original documents or proper verification receipts could result in immediate seat cancellation. Carry all original documents along with 3 sets of self-attested photocopies:

  • CET Cell CAP Registration Confirmation Acknowledgement Receipt
  • MHT-CET / JEE Main Scorecard (or Final Diploma Marksheet for DSE candidates)
  • SSC (Class 10th) & HSC (Class 12th) Marksheets and Passing Certificates
  • College Leaving Certificate / Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Domicile Certificate and Nationality Certificate (or Birth Certificate specifying Maharashtra birthplace)
  • Caste Certificate, Caste Validity Certificate, and Non-Creamy Layer Certificate (valid up to 31st March 2027), if applicable
  • Income Certificate and EWS Eligibility Certificate, if applicable
  • Migration Certificate (for out-of-state or CBSE/ICSE candidates)
  • Passport-sized photographs & Bank Demand Draft (DD) or active Net Banking for on-spot fee payment

Already Admitted in a Previous CAP Round? If your original certificates are retained by another institute where you secured a provisional seat, carry the Official Document Retention Certificate issued by that college along with attested photocopies and the original fee payment receipt.

8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can engineering colleges charge capitation fees or donations for Institute-Level rounds? ▼

No. The Maharashtra State CET Cell has mandated that Institute-Level admissions must be strictly merit-based. Charging donations, capitation fees, or engaging in under-the-table transactions is illegal and strictly prohibited under Maharashtra educational regulations.

Who is eligible to participate in Institute-Level and Against-CAP (ACAP) rounds? ▼

Candidates who registered and completed document verification through the CET Cell Centralized Admission Process (CAP) and either did not secure a seat, did not accept a previous allotment, or are seeking a better college/branch under vacancy seats are eligible to participate.

How will transparency be maintained during Institute-Level rounds in 2026? ▼

Colleges are required to submit daily admission reporting to the State CET Cell portal. Additionally, the government will appoint independent State Observers to monitor the admission process in colleges and curb any irregularities.

Do government scholarships (EBC, TFWS, Caste Freeship) apply to Institute-Level seats? ▼

Candidates admitted under the 20% Institutional Quota generally have to pay the full tuition fee determined by the Fee Regulating Authority (FRA) without government scholarship concessions. However, seats filled Against CAP (ACAP) vacancies may follow specific DTE/DBT guidelines depending on the candidate's admission status.

Should I pay an admission confirmation fee based on verbal assurance from a college agent? ▼

Never. Do not pay any amount or confirm admission solely on verbal assurances or through unauthorized middlemen. Always verify the college's official website for vacancy notices, published merit lists, and formal reporting schedules.

Where can students check official vacant seats for Institute-Level rounds? ▼

Official vacant seat matrices are published on the State CET Cell portal (cetcell.mahacet.org) post-CAP rounds, and detailed branch-wise vacancy lists must be displayed on the official websites and notice boards of the respective engineering colleges.
