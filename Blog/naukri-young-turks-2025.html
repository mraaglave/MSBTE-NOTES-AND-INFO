About the Contest

Naukri Campus Young Turks 2025 is a national-level Skill Assessment Test (SAT) for undergraduate students. The contest evaluates aptitude, problem-solving and domain-specific skills across two rounds. It’s free to participate and offers certificates, cash prizes, and industry recognition — perfect for building your CV and practicing placement-style assessments.

🗓️ Round 1: Aptitude Test — 20–21 Sep

⏱️ Format: 60 MCQs in 60 minutes

🔥 Top 30k students advance to Round 2

🗓️ Round 2: Skill Assessment — 4–5 Oct

🔢 Choose one skill track and attempt 60 MCQs in 60 minutes

🏅 Certificates for merit & participation

Referral & bonus: Bring friends onboard — referral pool of ₹2,00,000 to win additional cash prizes.

Round-wise Format

Round 1 — Aptitude

  • 60 Multiple Choice Questions
  • Duration: 60 minutes
  • Assess: pattern recognition, logical reasoning, quantitative aptitude
  • Top 30,000 students qualify for Round 2

Round 2 — Skill Assessment

Select one of the tracks below (available after qualifying Round 1). Format is 60 MCQs in 60 minutes.

Coding, Data Science & AI

Programming algorithms, data analysis, ML fundamentals, AI concepts.

Engineering

Core engineering topics across Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Chemical.

Accounting & Finance

Accounting concepts, finance principles, budgeting, commerce basics.

Sales & Marketing

Marketing strategies, sales operations, branding & communication skills.

Pharma

Pharmaceutical sciences, clinical research, drug development & regulations.
