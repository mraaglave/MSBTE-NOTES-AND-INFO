1. Important Session Timings (Regular vs. KT Exams)

Before you check your dates, you must understand the session timings. MSBTE conducts exams in two specific slots to manage regular and backlog students efficiently:

  • Regular Students: All regular semester exams will be conducted in the Morning Session (usually starting at 9:30 AM).
  • KT / Backlog Students: If you are appearing for a previous semester’s backlog paper, your exam will strictly be conducted in the Afternoon Session, which is scheduled from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

2. How to Download the Official Timetable

You can instantly view your personal branch-specific schedule on the official MSBTE portal. Follow these exact steps:

  • Visit the official MSBTE timetable link: https://online.msbte.co.in/timetable/
  • Once the page loads, you will see a search dashboard.
  • Select your Course Code (e.g., CO for Computer, AN for AI).
  • Select your Year/Semester.
  • Choose your Master Code / Scheme (Select K Scheme).
  • Click search, and your final day-wise timetable will be displayed on the screen.
