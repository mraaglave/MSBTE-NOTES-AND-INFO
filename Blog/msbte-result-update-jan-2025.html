Great news for all MSBTE diploma students! The Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE) has officially announced that the Winter 2025 Exam Result will be declared TODAY, 2nd January 2025 at 2:00 PM.

Students can check their results on the official MSBTE result portals. Make sure you have your Enrollment Number or Seat Number ready to check your result.

  • Result Declaration Date: 2nd January 2025 (TODAY)
  • Result Time: 2:00 PM
  • Exam Session: Winter 2025
  • Official Website: result.msbte.ac.in / msbte.ac.in

📱 How to Check MSBTE Winter 2025 Results

Follow these simple steps to check your result once it's available at 2:00 PM:

  1. Visit the official MSBTE result portal at result.msbte.ac.in
  2. Look for the link that says "Winter 2025 Exam Result" on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link to open the result search page.
  4. Enter your Enrollment Number or Seat Number carefully.
  5. Click on the "Show Result" or "Submit" button.
  6. Your result will be displayed on the screen. Take a screenshot or download the PDF.

📝 What to Check on Your Mark Sheet

After viewing your result, verify these important details:

  • Student Name: Ensure correct spelling
  • Enrollment Number: Should match your hall ticket
  • Subject-wise Marks: Check all subjects are listed
  • Total Marks: Verify the calculation
  • Result Status: Pass/Fail/ATKT/Condo

💡 Tip: If the website is slow or not loading, don't worry! This is common when results are freshly declared due to high traffic. Keep refreshing or try after a few minutes.
