Maharashtra Chief Minister Fellowship 2026: Apply Now for a Grade-A Level Opportunity

Published by MSBTE Notes & Info Team ∙ April 13, 2026 ∙ 6 min read

Are you a young professional looking to make a real impact on governance? The Maharashtra Chief Minister Fellowship Program (CMFP) 2026 is back, offering a unique bridge between youthful innovation and state administration with a ₹61,500 monthly stipend and an IIT Bombay Public Policy Course certificate.

Launched in 2015 by Hon. Shri Devendra Fadnavis, this 12-month program is widely considered one of the most prestigious fellowships in India. In this guide, we break down everything you need to know: from the ₹61,500 monthly stipend to the IIT Bombay Public Policy Course certification and the rigorous selection process.

🏛️ Key Highlights of the CM Fellowship 2026

Before diving into the details, here is a quick look at why thousands of graduates compete for this program every year:

🏅 Status: Position equivalent to a Grade-A Officer.
₹ Stipend: ₹56,100 + ₹5,400 (Travel Allowance) = ₹61,500 per month.
🎓 Education: A specialized Public Policy Certificate from IIT Bombay.
🤝 Mentorship: Work directly under District Collectors, CEOs, and senior Bureaucrats.
📅 Experience: 12 months of high-level administrative exposure.

✅ Eligibility Criteria: Do You Qualify?

To maintain a high standard of administrative support, the Maharashtra Government has set specific Chief Minister Fellowship eligibility requirements for the 2026 batch:

1. Age & Residency

  • Age Limit: Candidates must be between 21 and 26 years old.
  • Residency: You must be a Permanent Resident of Maharashtra.

2. Educational Background

  • Minimum Qualification: A Graduate degree from any discipline with at least 60% marks.
  • Language Proficiency: You must be able to read, write, and speak Marathi fluently. Proficiency in Hindi and English is also required.
  • Tech Savvy: Candidates must have a basic knowledge of computers and the internet.

3. Mandatory Work Experience

Applicants must have at least 1 year of full-time work experience.

💡 Note: Full-time internships, articleships (like CA), and even self-employment/entrepreneurship are considered valid experience as long as they meet the "48 hours per week" professional standard.

🎯 The Selection Process: How to Secure Your Spot

With only 60 seats available, the selection process for the CM Fellowship Maharashtra 2026 is designed to find the brightest minds. It consists of three main stages:

Stage 1: Online Objective Test

This 60-minute test covers:

  • General Knowledge: Current affairs, social issues, and Maharashtra's economy.
  • Reasoning & Aptitude: Logical thinking and quantitative data interpretation.
  • Language & IT: Marathi/English grammar and basic MS Office/Internet knowledge.

Stage 2: Essay Writing

The top 210 candidates from the objective test are invited to write three essays. These can be written in English, Marathi, or Hindi, focusing on policy-related topics.

Stage 3: Personal Interview

Shortlisted candidates will face a panel of senior officials to discuss their vision for Maharashtra's development and their suitability for the role.

💼 Roles and Responsibilities of a CM Fellow

What does a "Grade-A equivalent" role look like on the ground? As a fellow, your work will vary based on your placement (District Collector's office or a Government Department):

  • Policy Implementation: Monitoring the "last-mile delivery" of government schemes.
  • Data Analysis: Using your tech skills to provide data-driven insights to senior officials.
  • Innovative Projects: Designing and executing new initiatives to solve local administrative challenges.
  • Reporting: Submitting monthly progress reports to the Directorate of Economics and Statistics.

📝 How to Apply: Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Register: Visit the official Directorate of Economics and Statistics portal.
  2. Video Verification: You will need to record a short 15-second live video stating your name and location as part of the anti-fraud registration.
  3. Fill Details: Input your academic scores, work experience, and address.
  4. Pay Fee: An application fee of ₹500 is usually required to complete the submission.
  5. Submit: Download the PDF of your application for future reference.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is the CM Fellowship a permanent government job?

A: No. It is a one-time, 12-month program. It does not guarantee a permanent position, but it offers invaluable experience for those aiming for Civil Services or Public Policy careers.

Q: Can I choose my district for placement?

A: No. Placements are decided by the Directorate of Economics and Statistics based on state requirements.

Q: What is the benefit of the IIT Bombay Public Policy Course?

A: Fellows undergo a Public Policy training module curated by IIT Bombay. This provides a formal academic foundation to the practical work you do in the field, making your resume stand out globally.

Q: What is the stipend for the CM Fellowship Maharashtra 2026?

A: The fellowship provides ₹56,100 per month plus ₹5,400 travel allowance, totaling ₹61,500 per month.

Q: What is the age limit for CM Fellowship 2026?

A: Candidates must be between 21 and 26 years old and must be a permanent resident of Maharashtra.

🏆 Final Verdict

The CM Fellowship Maharashtra 2026 is the ultimate platform for engineers, graduates, and aspiring policymakers who want to see their ideas implemented at the state level. If you meet the Chief Minister Fellowship eligibility criteria, mark your calendars—this is your chance to lead Maharashtra into its next phase of growth.
