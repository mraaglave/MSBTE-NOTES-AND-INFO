When is the MSBTE Summer 2026 Result Expected?

The Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE) recently concluded the Summer 2026 examinations, which included both offline theory papers and the 6-phase online exam schedule. The last online exam (Phase 6) ended on 28 May 2026.

Historically, MSBTE follows a consistent evaluation timeline and usually declares results 25 to 30 days after the last examination is conducted. Based on this pattern, the MSBTE Summer 2026 result is expected to come out around the 3rd week of June 2026 (approximately 15–21 June).

Students who appeared across various K-Scheme and I-Scheme branches should keep this timeline in mind and ensure their enrollment numbers are readily available.

How to Check Your MSBTE Summer 2026 Result

Once the results are declared, follow these steps to check your score:

  1. Visit the official MSBTE result portal: msbte.org.in
  2. Look for the "Summer 2026 Examination Result" link in announcements.
  3. Enter your Enrollment Number or Seat Number.
  4. Solve the captcha and click Submit.
  5. Your result with subject-wise marks will be displayed.
  6. Download or print your digital marksheet for records.

⚠️ Server Crash Tip

On result day, the MSBTE website often slows down or crashes. If you can't access the site, try after 30 minutes or use a different browser. We'll also post direct mirror links on our WhatsApp group.

MSBTE Grace Marks & Passing Criteria

For MSBTE diploma exams under K-Scheme and I-Scheme, the minimum passing marks are 40% in each subject (theory and practical evaluated separately). If you fall short by a small margin, MSBTE's grace marks policy may help:

  • Condonation: Up to 5% grace marks may be applied per subject for students who fail in only 1-2 subjects.
  • ATKT Rules: Students who fail in up to 2 subjects can carry those papers and continue to the next semester.
  • Online Exam Subjects: As per rule RG11K, no photocopy or re-evaluation is available for online exam subjects.

What to Do After Result: Revaluation & Photocopy

If you are unsatisfied with your marks, MSBTE offers both photocopy (to view your answer sheet) and re-evaluation options. The window for applying typically opens within 7 days of result declaration.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

When is the MSBTE Summer 2026 result expected?

The MSBTE Summer 2026 result is expected to be declared in the 3rd week of June 2026 (approximately 15–21 June), based on the historical pattern of results coming 25-30 days after the last exam.

How many days does MSBTE take to declare results?

MSBTE typically declares results within 25 to 30 days after the last exam date. For Summer 2026, the last online exam was on 28 May, so expect results around mid-to-late June 2026.

Where can I check my MSBTE Summer 2026 result online?

You can check your result on the official MSBTE website at msbte.org.in by entering your enrollment number or seat number.

Is the MSBTE Summer 2026 result declared today?

As of the last update (May 29, 2026), the MSBTE Summer 2026 result has not been declared yet. It is expected in the 3rd week of June 2026. Stay connected for live updates.

How to download the MSBTE Summer 2026 marksheet?

After the result is declared, visit msbte.org.in, enter your enrollment or seat number, solve the captcha, and you can download or print your digital marksheet from the result page.

Can I apply for revaluation after the MSBTE Summer 2026 result?

Yes, you can apply for photocopy and re-evaluation for offline theory subjects within 7 days of the result. However, subjects conducted via online examination do not have a re-evaluation facility as per MSBTE rule RG11K.

What is the passing criteria for MSBTE diploma exams?

For MSBTE diploma exams under K-Scheme and I-Scheme, you need a minimum of 40% marks in each subject (theory and practical separately) to pass. Grace marks of up to 5% may apply per subject as per board rules.
