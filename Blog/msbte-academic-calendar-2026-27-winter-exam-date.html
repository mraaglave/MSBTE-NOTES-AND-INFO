1. Overview of MSBTE Academic Calendar 2026-27

The Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE) has officially issued the annual Academic Calendar for the session 2026-2027. This academic schedule governs all Government, Aided, and Unaided polytechnic institutes across Maharashtra offering diploma engineering and pharmacy programs under the outcome-based K-Scheme as well as the I-Scheme.

Staying up-to-date with term dates, class test windows, microproject submission deadlines, and board exam dates ensures students can plan their preparation systematically and avoid last-minute rush.

3. Winter 2026 Examination Form Submission

Students appearing for the MSBTE Winter 2026 examinations must complete online exam form submission through their respective institute student logins.

⚠️ Exam Form Deadlines & Requirements:

  • Regular Fee Form Filling: Scheduled during September 2026.
  • Late Fee Provision: Extended for 3-5 days after the normal fee deadline with an additional late charge.
  • Backlog Subjects: Students with backlog subjects must ensure all backlog codes are selected accurately.

4. K-Scheme Continuous Internal Marks Rules

Under the MSBTE K-Scheme curriculum, progressive assessment is compulsory across all engineering departments. Continuous evaluation includes:

  • Microprojects: Mandatory 10-mark individual or group assignment for every subject. Check our guide on Top 100 Engineering Project Ideas for Diploma Students.
  • Lab Manual Assessment: Completion of practical journals and continuous teacher signature logs.
  • Class Tests (CT-1 & CT-2): Mandatory attendance in both internal written tests.

6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

When does the MSBTE Odd Semester (2026-27) term start? ▼

For 3rd and 5th-semester students, the MSBTE Odd Semester academic term commences in July 2026. For newly admitted 1st-year (Sem 1) students, classes commence following the CAP allotment schedule in August 2026.

When will the MSBTE Winter 2026 theory examinations begin? ▼

According to the official MSBTE Academic Calendar 2026-27, the Winter 2026 theory examinations are scheduled to commence tentatively in November/December 2026.

When can students fill the MSBTE Winter 2026 examination form? ▼

The online examination form filling portal for MSBTE Winter 2026 will open in September 2026. Students must complete form filling through their institute portal before the normal fee deadline.

Are microprojects compulsory for MSBTE K-Scheme students? ▼

Yes, continuous internal assessment, including mandatory microprojects, lab manual assessments, and class tests, is compulsory under the MSBTE K-Scheme and I-Scheme curriculum.
