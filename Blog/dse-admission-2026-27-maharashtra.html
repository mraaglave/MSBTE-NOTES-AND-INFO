DSE Admission 2026-27 Maharashtra: Dates, Schedule, Registration & Eligibility

Published by MSBTE Notes & Info Team · July 20, 2026

The State CET Cell has released the official admission notice for Direct Second Year (Lateral Entry) Engineering / Technology for the Academic Year 2026-27. Get all the details regarding online registration, E-Scrutiny, CAP Rounds, and the complete admission schedule here.

⚡ TL;DR:

The registration for DSE Admission 2026-27 starts on 07-07-2026 and ends on 22-07-2026. Students must register online via mahacet.org, pay the fee (₹1300 for Open, ₹1000 for Reserved), and choose between E-Scrutiny and Physical Scrutiny for document verification. Make sure you don't miss the CAP Round dates!

The State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra, has issued the Admission Notice for Direct Second Year (Lateral Entry) - Engineering / Technology [3 Years Duration] for the Academic Year 2026-27. This applies to admissions in Government, Government Aided, University Managed, University Departments, and Unaided Private Professional Educational Institutions across Maharashtra.

If you are a student exploring what to do after diploma, pursuing an engineering degree is a highly recommended path. You might also be wondering about the difference between BE and BTech before finalizing your choices!

📄 Process of Application and Document Verification

The Candidate shall register online on www.mahacet.org, fill & submit the online application form, and upload the required documents. This year, the Competent Authority has introduced two modes for document verification:

  • E-Scrutiny Mode: You can upload documents from anywhere and they will be verified online by the designated E-Scrutiny Center. You don't need to visit personally.
  • Physical Scrutiny Mode: You need to select a time slot and visit the nearest Scrutiny Center (SC) / Facilitation Centre (FC) personally with original documents for verification.

⚠️ Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Application Rejection: If candidates fail to confirm online filled application by E-Scrutiny center or Physical Scrutiny Center, their names will not appear in the merit lists.
  • Seat Acceptance Fee: The fee is ₹ 1,000/- for all Candidates during the first seat acceptance. It is non-refundable and must be paid online.
  • Caste/Tribe Validity: Reserved category candidates (SC, VJ/DT NT, OBC, SBC, SEBC) must produce a "Caste Validity Certificate" and ST must submit a "Tribe Validity Certificate". If they submit a receipt during CAP registration, they must verify and submit the original before the final reporting date of the fourth CAP round.
  • Non Creamy Layer & EWS: All Backward Class candidates (excluding SC & ST) must produce a Non-Creamy Layer certificate valid up to 31st March 2027. EWS candidates must submit their Eligibility Certificate.
  • Option Form Freezing: Once an option form is confirmed online, it cannot be changed or canceled under any circumstances.
  • Auto Freeze: If you get your first preference in Round I (Auto Freeze) or top 3 preferences in Round II or top 6 in Round III, you MUST pay the seat acceptance fee and you won't be eligible for subsequent rounds.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is the registration fee for DSE Admission 2026?

A: The registration fee is ₹1300 for General category (Maharashtra/All India) and ₹1000 for Reserved Category, EWS, PWD, Orphan, and Transgender candidates.

Q: What are the modes of document verification?

A: Candidates can choose either E-Scrutiny (online verification without visiting a center) or Physical Scrutiny (visiting a Scrutiny Center personally) for document verification.

Q: What is the last date to register for DSE 2026?

A: The last date for online registration and document uploading for DSE 2026 is 22-07-2026 up to 05:00 PM.
