Why Participate in All India NCAT?

All India NCAT (Naukri Campus Aptitude Test) is India's leading free online aptitude test for college students. Over 4.7 Lakh students enrolled last year alone! It gives you a chance to benchmark your skills nationally, strengthen your aptitude for placements, and get noticed by top employers who increasingly value aptitude skills.

  • Higher Salaries: Students who score 70% or more in NCAT secure salaries that are 44% higher than their peers.
  • Real Insights: Receive a comprehensive performance report to uncover your strengths and find your improvement areas (topic-wise analysis).
  • Stand Out: A strong NCAT score can serve as a massive boost to your resume when applying for jobs or internships.
  • Compare with Peers: The test tells you where you rank among your course, year, and state.

Contest Format & Syllabus

The NCAT test is entirely stream-specific and based on real industry hiring patterns. Different streams have separate leaderboards, keeping the competition fair and highly relevant.

For Engineering Students

⏱️ 60 minutes, 60 MCQs

  • Quantitative Aptitude
  • Data Interpretation & Reasoning
  • Verbal Ability

For Non-Engineering Students

⏱️ 60 minutes, 45 MCQs + Speech Test

  • General Aptitude test
  • Interactive Speech assessment test
