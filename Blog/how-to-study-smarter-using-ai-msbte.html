Let us be honest — MSBTE syllabus is vast, time is short, and not every student has access to a good teacher for every subject. That gap is exactly where AI tools like Claude, ChatGPT, and Google Gemini step in. They are available 24/7, completely free to use, and can explain any topic in simple Marathi-friendly English.

Step 1: Choose the right AI tool

You do not need to pay anything to get started. All three major AI tools have a free tier that is more than enough for exam preparation:

1

Claude (claude.ai) — best for detailed explanations

Excellent for understanding theory, writing long answers in exam format, and breaking down complex concepts step by step. Best choice for subjects like Applied Mathematics, Physics, Engineering Drawing concepts.

2

ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) — great for quick answers

Good for fast Q&A, generating definitions, making notes summaries, and programming help for Computer/IT students. Free plan works well for most study needs.

3

Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) — best with Google account

Useful if you already use Gmail or Google Drive. Can read PDFs and your uploaded notes. Good for summarizing chapters quickly before exams.

Step 3: The right way to ask AI — prompt tips

Most students ask AI vague questions and get vague answers. The secret is to be specific. Compare these two prompts:

✕ Wrong way to ask

"Tell me about transistors"

— Too broad. You will get a 10-paragraph essay that is hard to use for exam prep.

✓ Right way to ask

"I am an MSBTE diploma Electronics student. Explain working of NPN transistor in simple language and write a 4-mark exam answer with diagram description."

— Specific, exam-ready, useful.

💡

Expert Prompting Techniques

✓ Ask for model answers

Tell AI: "Write a 6-mark MSBTE style answer on [topic] with proper headings and points." — This gives you a ready-to-memorize answer in exam format.

✓ Ask it to quiz you

Say: "Give me 5 MCQ questions on [chapter] with answers. Then ask me questions one by one and check my responses." — Turns AI into a practice test machine.

"Think of AI not as a search engine, but as a personal tutor who is always available, never judges you, and can explain the same concept 10 different ways until you get it."

Step 4: Smart daily study routine using AI

Morning — Understand

Read one chapter from your textbook first. Then ask AI to explain the parts you did not understand in simpler language.

Afternoon — Practise

Ask AI to give you numericals or short-answer questions from that chapter. Attempt them yourself first, then check with AI.

Evening — Revise

Ask AI: "Give me a 10-point summary of today's chapter in bullet points." Screenshot it and save for last-minute revision.

Night — Model Answers

Pick 2-3 important topics and ask AI to write model answers in MSBTE format. Read, understand, and try writing from memory.

⚠️ Important: Use AI wisely — do not misuse it

A word of caution for all students

AI is a study tool — not a shortcut to copy-paste answers without understanding. If you just copy AI answers without reading them, you will fail in the exam hall when you have to write from memory. Always read, understand, and then practise writing answers yourself. Use AI to learn, not to skip learning.

Also remember — AI can sometimes make mistakes, especially in technical numericals and very specific MSBTE syllabus details. Always cross-check important answers with your textbook or teacher.
