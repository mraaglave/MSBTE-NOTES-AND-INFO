Pune, 18th July 2026: Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU), commonly referred to as Pune University, has officially released the exam results for the Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) Final Year candidates. The announcement was made on 15th July 2026.

While final year students can now download their scorecards, the university has stated that results for the other years of engineering—First Year (F.E.), Second Year (S.E.), and Third Year (T.E.)—along with B.Tech degree programs will be updated and released sequentially in the upcoming weeks.

🌐 SPPU Result 2026 Official Website

Students can check their results through the official Savitribai Phule Pune University results portals listed below:

  • Official Website: unipune.ac.in
  • Result Dashboard: onlineresults.unipune.ac.in/SPPU

Note: If the website is loading slowly, please wait a few minutes and try again. Heavy traffic is common during result announcements.

🛠️ How to Check SPPU Results Online?

Students can follow these simple steps to check their SPPU Result 2026 online:

  1. Visit the official Pune University result portal: unipune.ac.in.
  2. Locate and click on the “Results” section.
  3. Enter your unique Seat Number / PRN Number and your Mother's Name exactly as written in your exam form.
  4. Type the Captcha Code correctly as displayed on your screen.
  5. Click the Check Result button to view and download your SPPU Result 2026 scorecard.

🔄 SPPU Revaluation Process

Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) provides students the option to apply for revaluation or rechecking if they are not satisfied with their exam results. After the declaration of results, the university releases an official notification for revaluation, re-totalling, and photocopy of answer sheets. Students must apply within a short window (usually a few days) through the official portal.

Who Can Apply?

  • Students who failed (ATKT / Fail status).
  • Students who passed but expect higher marks.

SPPU Fees Structure

  • Photocopy of Answer Sheet: INR 200 - 300 per subject
  • Revaluation: INR 500 - 1,000 per subject

The revaluation process involves a detailed reassessment of the answer sheet, and updated results are generally released within 2 - 4 weeks. If there is any change in marks, the revised score is treated as final and updated in academic records.

Steps to Apply for SPPU Revaluation:

  1. Apply for a photocopy of the answer sheet through the portal.
  2. Review your answer script carefully once received.
  3. Apply for revaluation through the official portal/college admin dashboard.
  4. Pay the required fees online.
  5. Wait for the updated result to be declared.
