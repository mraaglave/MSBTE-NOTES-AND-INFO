📅 Official MSBTE Summer 2026 Exam Date

According to the widely circulated academic calendar and recent notifications, the msbte summer time table 2026 is divided securely into two distinct phases. Students must complete their practical examinations before progressing to the offline written tests. Here is the officially documented msbte exam date summer 2026 schedule:

Examination Phase Confirmed Dates
👨‍🔬 Practical/Oral Examinations April 8, 2026 – April 18, 2026
✍️ Final Theory Examinations April 23, 2026 – May 16, 2026
📊 Expected Result Date 3rd Week of June 2026

🔍 How to Check Your Specific MSBTE Time Table Summer 2026?

The consolidated dates above indicate the overall window during which the state-board will conduct examinations. However, your specific msbte summer exam time table 2026 depends heavily on your branch (e.g., Computer, Civil, Mechanical) and your scheme (I-Scheme, K-Scheme). To find out your exact paper days:

  • Visit the official educational portal: msbte.org.in
  • Navigate to the 'Examination' module present on the top navigation bar.
  • Click on the prominently displayed link for the Summer 2026 Time Table.
  • Enter your Institute Code, Course Code (e.g., CO for Computer), Year/Semester, and Master Scheme.
  • A dynamic, subject-wise msbte time table will be generated. Download the precise PDF for your academic records.
