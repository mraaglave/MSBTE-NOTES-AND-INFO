🎉 MSBTE Result Winter 2026 (Winter 2025 Exam) Declared | Steps to Check

Published by Mr. Patil ∙ December 10, 2025

The wait is over! The MSBTE result 2026 winter (Winter 2025 exams) is expected in the second week of January 2026. Follow this guide to check your results immediately.

Keywords: msbte result winter 2026, msbte winter result 2026, check diploma result

The Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE) conducted the Winter session examinations from October to December 2025. Students have been eagerly searching for the MSBTE result winter 2026. The good news is that the MSBTE Winter 2025 results (also referred to as Winter 2026 result) are expected to be declared in the second week of January 2026.

Once announced, you can check your results directly on the official MSBTE website. This blog post guides you through the process of checking your result, downloading your mark sheet, and what to verify on your report card.

Key Highlights

  • Exam Session: Winter 2025 (Oct - Dec)
  • Expected Result Date: 2nd Week of January 2026
  • Official Website: msbte.ac.in / msbte.org.in

How to Check MSBTE Winter 2025 Results

Follow these simple steps to access your results once they are available online:

  1. Visit the official MSBTE result portal at msbte.org.in or the main website.
  2. Look for the notification link that says "Winter 2025 Exam Result" or "Winter 2025 Diploma Result" on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link to open the result search page.
  4. Enter your Enrollment Number or Seat Number into the provided input fields carefully.
  5. Click on the "Show Result" or "Download PDF" button.
  6. Your result will be displayed on the screen. View it and click "Print" or "Save as PDF" to download your mark sheet for future records.

Important Details on Your Mark Sheet

After downloading your digital mark sheet, it is crucial to verify that all the information is correct. Check the following details:

  • Student Name: Ensure the spelling matches your official documents.
  • Enrollment Number and Seat Number: Verify these match your hall ticket.
  • Course Name and Subject Names: Check if all your appeared subjects are listed correctly.
  • Subject-wise Marks and Total Marks: Tally the marks to ensure there are no totaling errors.
  • Result Status: Check the final status (Pass/Fail/ATKT/Condo).

Note: If you notice any discrepancies like incorrect marks, name spelling errors, or if your result shows 'RHR' (Result Held in Reserve), contact your institute or principal immediately.

Revaluation and Photocopy

If you are not satisfied with your marks, or if you believe there has been an error in assessment, MSBTE provides an option for Revaluation or requesting a Photocopy of Answer Book.

You will need to apply for these services through the official website within a specific timeframe (usually within 7-10 days of result declaration). Keep an eye on the official circulars for the schedule of Photocopy & Reassessment.

FAQ — MSBTE Winter 2025 Results

Q: When will the MSBTE result winter 2026 (Winter 2025 session) be declared?

A: The results are expected to be announced in the second week of January 2026 based on the usual schedule.

Q: How can I check my result if I forgot my credentials?

A: You generally need your Seat Number or Enrollment Number. These are available on your Hall Ticket. If you lost your Hall Ticket, contact your college.

Q: What is a "Condo" in the result status?

A: "Condo" stands for Condonation. It means you passed the subject with grace marks awarded by the board as per the rules.
