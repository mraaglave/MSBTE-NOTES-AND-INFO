Published by MSBTE Notes & Info Team · April 14, 2026

Everything you need to know about MSBTE Summer 2026 online exam login credentials, Shika Va Kamva (Earn While Learn) exam schedule, and State Government approved short-duration program subject codes.

How to Get Your Online Exam Login Credentials

Students appearing for the MSBTE Summer 2026 online examinations must have their unique User ID and Password to access the exam portal. As per MSBTE Circular No. 42, the distribution of these credentials will take place through your respective institutions. For a full list of subjects requiring these credentials, see the MSBTE Online Exam Subject Codes.

Important Dates for Credentials:

  • 21 April 2026: Institution-wise candidate list available to exam centres.
  • 4 May 2026: Distribution of Student User IDs and Passwords begins.

We recommend contacting your college exam coordinator or department head after 4 May 2026 to collect your login details. Don't wait until the last day! Practice logging in at least 2–3 days before your actual exam date to ensure your institution's internet and your credentials work correctly.

Shika Va Kamva (Earn While Learn) – Online Exam Schedule

The board also conducts online exams for students enrolled under the Shika Va Kamva (Earn While Learn) scheme. These exams are separate and have a dedicated window. For Summer 2026, the exam date is fixed from 18 May to 21 May 2026. This schedule aligns with the main MSBTE Summer 2026 Online Exam Timetable.

State Government Short-Duration Programs (Alpa Mudati)

Students enrolled in state government approved short-duration programs (6 months or less) also have their theory exams in online mode. These exams are also scheduled between 18 May and 21 May 2026. The relevant subject code series for these programs generally start with 281xx and 282xx.

Important Rules – No Photocopy or Re-evaluation

As per MSBTE Rule RG11K, photocopy and re-evaluation facilities are not provided for online examinations. Since the evaluation is computerized and immediate, there is no manual re-checking process. Students are advised to be very careful while selecting answers during the exam.
