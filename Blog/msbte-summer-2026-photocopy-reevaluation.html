Overview: What's New for Summer 2026?

The Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE) has officially released the schedule and guidelines for obtaining answer sheet photocopies and applying for Re-Evaluation of Summer 2026 examination results. Students who are dissatisfied with their marks can use these facilities through the Digital Evaluation system.

If you have already checked your result and feel your marks don't reflect your performance, here is everything you need to know to apply for a photocopy and/or re-evaluation. Make sure you've already checked your MSBTE Summer 2026 Result before proceeding with the photocopy process. You may also want to read our detailed Revaluation & Grace Marks Rules guide for a better understanding of how the process works.

Important Dates: Photocopy & Re-Evaluation Schedule

To ensure you don't miss the deadlines, keep a close eye on these critical dates:

Phase 1: Photocopy Application

Activity Date Range
Student Application for Photocopy (via Student Login) 20.06.2026 to 22.06.2026
Institute Verification & Regional Office Processing After 22.06.2026
Photocopy Available Online (to Student Login) 02.07.2026

Phase 2: Re-Evaluation (Re-Assessment)

Activity Date Range
Student Application for Re-Evaluation (via Student Login) 03.07.2026 to 04.07.2026
Online Declaration of Re-Evaluation Result 15.07.2026

Digital Evaluation: Why No Mark Verification?

MSBTE has clarified that under the Digital Evaluation system, answer sheets are already evaluated electronically and marks are calculated automatically by computer. This eliminates the possibility of human error in totaling or mark entry.

  • No "Verification" (Gun-Padtalni): Since the digital system ensures precise calculation of marks, the traditional mark verification or totaling verification facility is not available under this system.
  • Available Services: Students can only apply for a Photocopy of their evaluated answer sheet and/or Re-Evaluation (re-assessment). This ensures complete transparency in the Maharashtra technical education board's evaluation process.

Step-by-Step Application Guide

The entire process is done online through the official MSBTE portal. Follow these steps carefully:

  1. Login: Visit the official MSBTE website and go to Examination Tab > Digital Evaluation > Student Login. Use your Enrollment Number and any one Seat Number from the Summer 2026 exam to sign in.
  2. Apply for Photocopy First: Select the subjects for which you want a photocopy. Complete the online application and pay the prescribed fee during the window of 20 June to 22 June 2026.
  3. Download Fee Receipt: After submitting the online form, download the fee receipt and submit the required documents to your institute within the specified period.
  4. Review Your Photocopy: Photocopies will be made available online on 2 July 2026. Carefully review your answer sheet for any discrepancies.
  5. Apply for Re-Evaluation (if needed): If you find that your answers have not been evaluated correctly, apply for Re-Evaluation between 3 July to 4 July 2026.
  6. Check Re-Evaluation Result: The final Re-Evaluation result will be declared online on 15 July 2026.
Pro Tip: Always obtain a photocopy of your answer sheet before applying for Re-Evaluation. Review where you lost marks so you can make an informed decision about whether Re-Evaluation is worth it.

Critical Instructions to Remember

⚠️ Important: Applications submitted after the deadline or without proper fee payment will not be accepted. Make sure you complete all steps within the specified period.

  • Photocopy Before Re-Evaluation: MSBTE strongly recommends obtaining a photocopy first. If marks change after reviewing the photocopy, students can then proceed with Re-Evaluation.
  • Finality of Marks: The marks awarded after Re-Evaluation will be treated as final and binding, even if they are lower than your original marks.
  • Complete All Steps: Students must complete the online application, pay the prescribed fee, download the fee receipt, and submit the required documents within the specified period.
  • Regular Portal Checks: Students are advised to regularly check the MSBTE Student Login portal and follow all deadlines carefully to avoid missing the opportunity.

FAQ — MSBTE Summer 2026 Photocopy & Re-Evaluation

When is the last date to apply for a photocopy?

The student application window for photocopy is open from 20 June 2026 to 22 June 2026. Don't wait until the last minute!

When will the photocopy be available?

After verification by institutes and regional offices, photocopies will be made available online on 2 July 2026 through the Student Login portal.

Can I apply for Re-Evaluation directly without a photocopy?

MSBTE recommends a two-phase approach: first obtain a photocopy to review your answer sheet, and then apply for Re-Evaluation if you find discrepancies. However, check your student login for specific options based on current rules.

Can I apply for Mark Verification (Gun-Padtalni)?

No. Due to the Digital Evaluation system which ensures precise calculation, the traditional "Verification" facility is not available. You can only apply for Photocopy and/or Re-Evaluation.

What if my marks decrease after Re-Evaluation?

As per MSBTE rules, the re-evaluated marks are treated as final, even if they are lower than your original score. Apply only if you are confident about discrepancies.

When will the Re-Evaluation result be declared?

The final Re-Evaluation results will be declared online on 15 July 2026.
